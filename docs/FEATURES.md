# Features

## Game
- Case briefing with animated file-opening sequence
- Illustrated greenhouse scene with 8 hotspots; healthy vs sick plants are visible
- Evidence reveal sheet, XP toast, level-up celebration
- Witness chat with suggested questions, free-text questions and "present evidence"
- Evidence board: tap two clues to test a connection; wrong guesses get a nudge, not the answer
- Hypothesis writer with evidence citation; rubric review with per-criterion feedback
- Case-closed sequence: stamp, debrief, deduction report, XP count-up, level progress, achievements

## Lab
- pH probe with settling animation and a pH scale
- Nutrient availability model (9 elements) updated live as you drag the pH slider; scarce/toxic shown by icon and text
- Fertilizer acidity comparison
- Controlled lime trial: treated vs untreated control, vigor index (Liebig's law of the minimum), over-liming penalty (Pro)

## Progression
XP, 20 levels with 11 ranks, achievements, knowledge nodes. XP is granted once per discovery (no farming).

## Platform
- 5-tab mobile shell, safe areas, bottom sheets, skeletons, haptics on native
- Offline/timeout/AI-down/RevenueCat-down states with retry
- In-app account deletion

## Known limits
- Only one authored flagship case. AI-generated cases open in the classic (non-mobile-polished) screen
- Legacy screens (multiplayer, teacher tools, leaderboard, missions, onboarding) were not redesigned
